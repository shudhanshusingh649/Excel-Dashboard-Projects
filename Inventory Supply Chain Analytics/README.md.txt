# Inventory & Supply Chain Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing inventory and supply chain data using Microsoft Excel.

The objective of this project is to transform raw supply chain data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, calculated columns, PivotTables, KPIs, and interactive visualizations.

The final dashboard provides a consolidated view of order performance, supplier risk, transport modes, warehouse load, and lead-time analysis.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall order performance
- Evaluate supplier performance and delay risk
- Analyze order quantity across different transport modes
- Identify top suppliers based on order quantity
- Analyze average lead time by transport mode
- Analyze seasonal variations in lead time
- Understand warehouse load distribution
- Create a professional management dashboard
- Present important supply chain KPIs in a clear visual format

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel

## 📈 Key KPIs

The dashboard includes the following major KPIs:

- Total Orders
- Average Delay History
- Total Order Quantity
- Average Order Quantity
- Average Warehouse Load

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the overall supply chain performance.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Supplier Delay Risk

Shows the average historical delay percentage for suppliers.

**Insight:**  
This visualization helps identify suppliers with relatively higher historical delay risk.

---

### 2. Order Quantity by Transport Mode

Compares the total order quantity handled through:

- Air
- Ship
- Train
- Truck

**Insight:**  
This helps understand how order volume is distributed across different transportation modes.

---

### 3. Top 10 Suppliers by Order Quantity

Displays the top 10 suppliers based on total order quantity.

**Insight:**  
This helps identify suppliers contributing the highest order volumes.

---

### 4. Average Lead Time by Season

Compares average lead time across:

- Monsoon
- Normal
- Peak

**Insight:**  
This helps analyze whether seasonal conditions influence supply chain lead time.

---

### 5. Warehouse Load Distribution

Shows the distribution of orders across warehouse load categories:

- Critical
- High
- Normal

**Insight:**  
This helps identify the overall warehouse workload distribution and potential capacity pressure.

---

### 6. Average Lead Time by Transport

Compares average lead time across different transport modes.

**Insight:**  
This visualization helps compare transportation modes based on delivery lead time.

---

### 7. Orders by Transport Mode

Shows the number of orders handled by each transport mode.

**Insight:**  
This helps understand the operational distribution of orders across transportation methods.

---

## 📌 Key Business Insights

The dashboard helps answer important supply chain questions such as:

- Which suppliers have higher historical delay risk?
- Which suppliers contribute the highest order quantity?
- Which transport mode handles the highest order volume?
- Which transport mode has the highest average lead time?
- How does lead time vary across different seasons?
- What is the current distribution of warehouse load?
- What is the overall order and inventory performance?

## 🖥️ Dashboard

The final Excel dashboard consolidates KPIs and visualizations into a single management-friendly view.

The dashboard is designed to provide a quick overview of supply chain performance and support data-driven decision making.
