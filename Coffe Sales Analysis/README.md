# ☕ Coffee Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Coffee Sales Dashboard created in Microsoft Excel** to analyze coffee shop sales performance and identify important business insights.

The dashboard provides a visual summary of sales using KPIs, charts, and interactive filters.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Slicers
* Excel Formulas
* Data Visualization

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes three main KPIs:

* **Total Sales:** 112,245.58
* **Average Transaction Value:** 31.65
* **Total Transactions:** 3,547

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Sales by Coffee

This chart compares sales performance across different coffee products, including:

* Espresso
* Cortado
* Cocoa
* Hot Chocolate
* Americano
* Cappuccino
* Americano with Milk
* Latte

### 2. Sales by Day

Shows the total sales generated on each day of the week and helps identify the best-performing days.

### 3. Monthly Sales

Displays the monthly sales trend from January to December and helps identify changes in sales performance throughout the year.

### 4. Hourly Sales

Shows sales performance by hour and helps identify the peak business hours.

### 5. Payment Method

Displays the distribution of transactions according to the payment method.

### 6. Sales by Time of Day

Categorizes sales into:

* Morning
* Afternoon
* Night

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard contains interactive slicers for:

* **Weekday**
* **Year**

Users can apply these filters to analyze sales for specific days and years.

## 💡 Business Insights

This dashboard can help businesses:

* Identify the best-selling coffee products.
* Find the highest-performing days.
* Analyze monthly sales trends.
* Identify peak sales hours.
* Understand customer payment preferences.
* Compare sales across different times of the day.
* Make better decisions regarding inventory and staffing.
